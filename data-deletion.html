1. Request deletion of billing data

InvoiceNest: Invoice Maker is published by Dark Wings LLC. To request deletion of RevenueCat customer data associated with this app, email darkwingsllc@gmail.com with the subject “InvoiceNest data deletion”. You do not need an InvoiceNest or website account to read these instructions or contact us.

Include your Billing support ID from InvoiceNest Settings, if available, and specify that you are requesting deletion of your InvoiceNest billing data. This helps identify the anonymous RevenueCat customer record. If you no longer have the app or ID, explain that in your email. We may need additional information to identify the record and verify the request; your email address alone does not identify an anonymous billing profile.

Do not email passwords, full payment-card details, customer lists, or invoice backups. The app does not upload invoice contents to RevenueCat or a publisher invoice server.

2. What the request covers

RevenueCat customer deletion covers the identified customer profile and its associated purchase history in RevenueCat. This includes billing identifiers and subscription records associated with that profile. The publisher cannot use that operation to erase Google’s independently held payment/store records, remove exports from other services, or erase local data from your phone remotely.

InvoiceNest has no scheduled expiry for remote billing profiles. RevenueCat and Google control retention in their own systems under their applicable policies. No fixed period for deletion from all provider backups is represented here. Returning to the app or restoring purchases can create or associate a billing record again.

3. Remove records from your device

To remove app-managed invoices, customers, business details, monthly allowance entries, recovery copies, and cache files, open Android Settings → Apps → InvoiceNest → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage, or uninstall InvoiceNest. Android menu names vary. Export records you need before clearing storage; clearing it removes the installation’s local records. This is Android-managed removal, not a promise of forensic secure erasure.

Voiding an invoice does not delete it. Editing a customer does not erase invoice snapshots. Copies you saved or shared as PDF, CSV, or JSON outside the app must be deleted separately in the receiving app, file location, email service, or cloud storage.

4. Cancel a subscription separately

Uninstalling InvoiceNest, clearing its storage, or deleting a RevenueCat customer does not cancel a Google Play subscription. To stop renewals, use Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → InvoiceNest → Cancel subscription. Cancellation and a request to delete billing data are separate actions.
