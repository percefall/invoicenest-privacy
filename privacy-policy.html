1. About this policy

This policy explains how InvoiceNest handles information when you create invoices and use its Android subscription features. InvoiceNest is an invoice-making app with local records and optional Pro subscriptions. Dark Wings LLC is the publisher. Privacy questions and subscription-data requests can be sent to darkwingsllc@gmail.com.

There is no InvoiceNest account registration, email sign-in, password, social login, or cloud invoice account. Google Play separately manages the Google account used for purchases. RevenueCat creates a pseudonymous billing identifier; this is not an InvoiceNest login and is not guaranteed to identify you by name.

2. Information you enter and create

On your device, InvoiceNest processes and stores the business name, email address, phone number, postal address, and payment instructions you enter; customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, and postal addresses; and invoice numbers, record identifiers, business/customer snapshots, issue and due dates, line-item descriptions, quantities, prices, taxes, totals, currency, notes, and draft/sent/paid/void status. Records also contain creation/update times and internal revision/numbering information. Payment instructions and notes may contain bank details or other personal information if you choose to enter them.

This information is used to prepare, calculate, save, find, display, and export your invoices. Invoice snapshots preserve the business and customer details used for that invoice, even if you later edit the separate profile or customer record. Marking an invoice paid is a manual record; InvoiceNest does not collect your customers’ payments or verify that payment occurred.

InvoiceNest also keeps monthly counts of newly saved invoices to apply the Free allowance. Those counts stay on the device and are not included in exported JSON invoice backups. Search/filter text is used in the app interface; it is not sent to a search service.

InvoiceNest does not upload your entered business/customer fields, invoice contents, bank instructions, or invoice backup files to RevenueCat or a publisher-operated invoice server.

3. Device storage and backups

Android records are stored in the app’s private SQLite database. Pre-restore recovery copies are stored as JSON files in the app’s document directory. Generated JSON/CSV/PDF exports and selected import files may also remain in the app’s cache. The app validates imported backups and creates a local recovery copy before replacing invoice records.

InvoiceNest does not provide automatic invoice cloud backup or synchronization. Android backup is disabled in the application manifest; device transfer behavior can still depend on Android and the device manufacturer. InvoiceNest does not add its own database encryption, file encryption, or app passcode. Android provides the app sandbox and device-level protections; protect access to your device.

4. Files you import, export, or share

When you choose backup export, CSV export, or PDF export, InvoiceNest generates the file locally and opens the Android share interface. The receiving app, person, file location, email provider, or cloud service is selected by you. InvoiceNest does not automatically email invoices, choose a recipient, or upload these files to its own cloud service. A file provider you select during import may retrieve a file from its own cloud storage.

JSON backups contain your saved business profile, customers, and invoices, including historical snapshots. CSV files contain invoice summaries and notes; PDFs contain the displayed invoice and contact/payment details. InvoiceNest does not encrypt these exported files. Copies saved or sent outside the app are controlled by you and the destination, and are subject to that service’s practices. Sharing completion does not prove that a destination saved the file.

5. Google Play and RevenueCat subscriptions

The configured Android app connects to RevenueCat at startup, including when you use the Free plan without purchasing Pro. It also checks access when the app returns to the foreground and during subscription/allowance actions. There is currently no in-app switch to disable these configured billing connections.

The RevenueCat SDK creates and caches an anonymous App User ID and sends that identifier to RevenueCat. Billing processing also involves Google Play purchase tokens and transaction/order metadata, product/base-plan identifiers, purchase history, price/currency information, and subscription/entitlement state such as renewal, expiration, cancellation, and refund status. Google Play and RevenueCat use this information to process and validate purchases, restore access, maintain Pro status, and provide subscription reporting/analytics to the publisher.

SDK requests also include technical information such as app/package and SDK versions, Android version, device model/brand, language/locale preferences, store/storefront, and technical request/build/background-state information. The service receives network connection information, including the IP address needed to receive a request. RevenueCat documents country information derived from transactions or a last-seen IP address, and connection timestamps. Its customer-profile documentation says the IP used for country derivation is dropped after that derivation. This is not access to your device’s GPS or precise location.

InvoiceNest does not supply your business/customer name, email, phone, address, or invoice data as RevenueCat customer attributes. Automatic device-identifier collection is disabled in the SDK configuration, and the app does not call advertising-identifier or attribution collection. The billing identifier and necessary subscription traffic still exist.

Google Play handles the store payment interface and payment method under Google’s policies. InvoiceNest does not receive or store your payment-card number through that purchase flow. This does not prevent you from entering financial details yourself in local invoice fields.

Google Play purchase updates are delivered through Google Cloud Pub/Sub to RevenueCat for subscription status processing. This is a billing notification channel, not invoice cloud storage. We do not forward this data to other analytics or marketing services through RevenueCat integrations.

6. Analytics, crash reporting, and advertising

InvoiceNest has no separate general-purpose analytics, advertising, session replay, or crash-reporting SDK configured. It has no Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, or advertising SDK. RevenueCat subscription analytics and SDK technical metadata are described in section 5.

The installed React Native purchases SDK defaults its optional diagnostics flag to false, and InvoiceNest does not enable it. Android or Google Play may separately process store/device diagnostics under their own settings and policies. Local error messages are displayed by the app and are not uploaded by an application crash-reporting service.

There is no configured app feature that sells invoice records, sends them to advertisers, or uses them for advertising profiling.

7. Services and security

RevenueCat provides subscription validation, entitlement management, billing records, and subscription analytics. Google Play/Google provide store billing and purchase notifications; Google Cloud Pub/Sub carries those notifications. Expo/React Native modules provide local storage, file selection, PDF generation, and sharing. Their presence does not mean that InvoiceNest uploads invoice data to Expo. Over-the-air Expo updates are disabled in the Android app.

Billing service communications use HTTPS. Local database and exported/recovery files are not separately encrypted by InvoiceNest. Do not send invoice backups or bank details to support unless specifically needed and requested through a verified channel. No application can promise absolute security.

Google and RevenueCat process subscription information on their systems under their applicable policies and service arrangements. Their infrastructure may be outside your state or country. InvoiceNest does not select a single storage country or implement a separate retention schedule for those providers. Provider privacy information is linked below.

8. Retention and deletion

Local invoice/customer/profile records and monthly allowance entries have no automatic age-based deletion. The app does not currently offer individual record deletion or an erase-all screen. Voiding an invoice keeps it in the records. Editing a customer or business profile does not erase historical invoice snapshots.

Successful pre-restore recovery files are retained without automatic pruning, so an earlier version of your records may remain after a restore. Cached imported files and generated exports are not systematically deleted after use; Android may clear cache when needed. Editing or replacing current records does not guarantee erasure of older database pages, recovery files, or cache copies.

To remove this installation’s local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → InvoiceNest → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Menu names vary by device. Export anything you need first. This removes app-managed local copies through Android; it does not promise forensic secure erasure or remove files you saved/shared elsewhere. Remove those external copies separately with the relevant recipient or service.

Clearing app data or uninstalling does not cancel a Google Play subscription and does not automatically erase store/RevenueCat purchase history. Manage or cancel renewals in Google Play’s subscription settings. Reinstallation may create a new anonymous billing identifier; restoring a purchase can associate access with store purchase records again.

InvoiceNest does not implement automatic age-based deletion of RevenueCat customer records. Billing identifiers, subscription status and purchase history remain in the billing service until removed through its customer-deletion tools or handled under the provider’s own retention rules. Google separately controls the retention of its store and payment records. This policy does not promise a fixed provider-wide retention period or that a publisher request erases Google’s records.

To request deletion of RevenueCat customer data associated with InvoiceNest, email darkwingsllc@gmail.com with the subject “InvoiceNest data deletion”. Include your Billing support ID from InvoiceNest Settings, if available, and say which data you want removed. If you no longer have the installation or ID, explain that in your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, invoice backups, or other customers’ contact information. The anonymous billing ID is not your name or email; additional information may be needed to identify the correct record and verify the request. We cannot identify an anonymous billing record from an email address alone.

RevenueCat provides a Delete Customer operation for removing an identified customer profile and its purchase history. A deletion request is separate from cancellation of a Google Play subscription. Returning to the app or restoring purchases can create or associate a billing record again while the SDK is in use. The data-deletion instructions explain removal of local records, external exports, and provider records separately. No fixed completion deadline or deletion from every provider backup is promised by this policy.

9. Android permissions

The app declares INTERNET for billing connections, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for network availability, com.android.vending.BILLING for Google Play purchases, and VIBRATE inherited from the runtime (no app vibration feature was found). It also declares a signature-protected internal dynamic-receiver permission used by Android components; this is not access to your personal records.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, call-log, broad storage, notification, or advertising-ID permissions. File import/export uses the system picker/share interfaces for the file you select. The app does not show runtime permission prompts.

10. Contact, audience, and policy changes

Dark Wings LLC is responsible for InvoiceNest. Privacy and subscription-data requests: darkwingsllc@gmail.com. InvoiceNest has no user-created account. A request concerning subscription data is separate from removal of local invoice records and from cancellation of a store subscription.

InvoiceNest is intended for adults aged 18 and over, consistent with its Google Play target-audience setting. Its initial target market is the United States. The app does not implement age verification. If you believe a child has provided information through the app or its billing services, contact darkwingsllc@gmail.com without sending the child’s invoice records.

This policy is effective October 29, 2026. Changes to app functionality or service settings may require updates to this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration. You may contact us about privacy questions, access, correction, or deletion requests; any applicable rights depend on your jurisdiction and the records we can identify.

If you email a privacy request, the message may include your email address, billing identifier and the information you choose to provide. These are processed through the email services involved, including Gmail for the published contact address, to handle your request. Do not include invoice backups or other customers’ personal information. Emailing support is separate from the app’s automatic billing connections.
